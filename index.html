Disposal Guide

Dry Waste

Plastic Bottle

How to Dispose

Rinse and place in the blue recycling bin. Remove caps if they're a different material.

Where to Dispose

Local recycling centers or municipal blue bins in your area.

Recycling Potential

Highly recyclable! This item can be turned into new products.

Can You Sell It?

Yes! Clean plastic bottles can be sold to scrap dealers for ₹5-10 per kg.

What NOT to Do

  • ❌ Don't mix with wet waste
  • ❌ Don't burn plastic items
  • ❌ Don't throw in water bodies

Waste Management Information

Wet Waste

Biodegradable waste like food scraps, vegetable peels, and garden waste. Compost it to create nutrient-rich soil!

Dry Waste

Non-biodegradable items like plastic, paper, metal, and glass. Recycle whenever possible to reduce landfill waste.

E-Waste

Electronic items like phones, batteries, and appliances. Contains toxic materials - never dispose with regular trash!

Hazardous Waste

Chemicals, medicines, paints, and other dangerous materials. Requires special handling and disposal facilities.
